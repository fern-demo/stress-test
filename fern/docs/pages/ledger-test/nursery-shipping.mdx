## Packaging requirements

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the plant">
    Remove excess soil and wrap roots in damp paper towel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure the pot">
    Place the plant in a sturdy box with packing material to prevent movement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label the package">
    Apply "LIVE PLANTS" and "THIS SIDE UP" labels to all sides.
  </Step>
</Steps>
