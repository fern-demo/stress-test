## Plant care resources

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Light requirements">
    Most indoor plants thrive in bright, indirect light. South-facing windows provide the most light, while north-facing windows offer gentler, indirect illumination.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Watering guidelines">
    The frequency of watering depends on the plant species, pot size, and environmental conditions. Always check the top inch of soil before watering.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Soil and fertilizer">
    Use well-draining potting mix for most houseplants. Fertilize during the growing season (spring and summer) with a balanced liquid fertilizer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
