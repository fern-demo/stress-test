## Light levels

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Low light">
    Plants like pothos, snake plants, and ZZ plants thrive in low-light conditions. Place them in rooms with north-facing windows or away from direct sunlight.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Medium light">
    Most ferns, philodendrons, and peace lilies prefer medium, indirect light. East or west-facing windows work well.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bright indirect light">
    Monstera, fiddle leaf fig, and rubber plants need bright but filtered light. South-facing windows with sheer curtains are ideal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Temperature and humidity

Most houseplants prefer temperatures between 60-80°F (15-27°C) and humidity levels of 40-60%.

<Tip>
  Group plants together to create a microclimate with higher humidity.
</Tip>
