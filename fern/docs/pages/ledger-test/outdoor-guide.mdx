## Climate zones

| Zone | Min temp | Example plants |
|------|----------|---------------|
| 3-4 | -40 to -20°F | Hostas, daylilies |
| 5-6 | -20 to 0°F | Roses, lavender |
| 7-8 | 0 to 20°F | Gardenias, camellias |
| 9-10 | 20 to 40°F | Citrus, bougainvillea |
| 11+ | 40°F+ | Tropical palms, plumeria |

## Soil preparation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test soil pH">
    Most plants prefer a pH between 6.0 and 7.0. Use a soil testing kit from your local garden center.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amend the soil">
    Add compost or peat moss to improve drainage and nutrient content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mulch the surface">
    Apply 2-3 inches of organic mulch to retain moisture and suppress weeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
